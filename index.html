About

I started by automating my own job.
Then I found the bigger problem.

My day job is Marketing & Export Operations at an Indonesian commodity exporter. Doing the same manual workflow every week is what got me writing Python on the side — first a pipeline that builds shipment documents end-to-end, now in daily use.

But the pipeline that taught me the most wasn't about documents. It was a small system that catches buyer inquiries before they slip through — and that's when it clicked. The expensive problem for most businesses isn't slow paperwork. It's the customer who was ready to buy, lost somewhere between "they messaged us" and "nobody got back to them." That leak doesn't show up in any report. The money just quietly disappears.

So that's what I build now: AI Lead Rescue Systems for service businesses — agencies, consultants, contractors, clinics, studios. Capture every inquiry, draft a reply in minutes, follow up until it closes. Stack is plain — Python, Claude API, n8n — because it has to actually work, not just demo well.

Bachelor's in Informatics Engineering, Cumlaude 3.56/4.00, from Universitas Dian Nuswantoro. Internship at PT Kereta Api Indonesia (Daop 4 Semarang) building a warehouse management system. Based in Bandung. Remote everywhere.
