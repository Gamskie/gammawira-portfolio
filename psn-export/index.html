Why our briquette holds quality

14-day cooling/weathering process

Every batch sits a minimum 14 days at controlled temperature (<40°C) in open-air storage under roof before packing. Standard SOP since day one — recorded in cooling reports per batch. Result: stable burn, low ash, no surprise humidity defects on arrival.

Zero chemical, natural only

Mix is exactly three ingredients: coconut charcoal powder + water + tapioca powder. Nothing else. Raw material sourced direct from private suppliers in Sulawesi — single-origin, single-grade, screened before milling.

Automated production line

Drying, screening, grinding, mixing, molding, oven — all automatic machinery. Consistency over batch-by-batch handmade. 12.5 ton/day total capacity, oven 2.5 tons/cycle. Daily QC reports per supervisor.

200 local employees, factory in Blora

Direct manufacturer (not trader). Factory Jl. Raya Blora-Cepu KM. 14, Jiken — ~70 km from Tanjung Emas loading port. 200 employees, mostly local. Visit welcomed for serious buyers.
