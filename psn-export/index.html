How we work

1. Inquiry → Proforma

Send us your specs: target volume, destination port, brand/variant preference, packaging. We send a proforma within 24-48 hours with current FOB rate.

2. Trial container

First-time buyers — we recommend a single FCL to validate fit. DP 30-50% standard. Full document set provided (UN1361, MSDS, Phyto, etc.) so customs goes smoothly.

3. Repeat orders

Volume customers get production slot reservation, fresh proforma each cycle, and direct line to operations team. References available for buyers shipping 4+ FCL/year.

4. Custom packaging

Private label, custom carton design, brand printing — we work with your specs. Lead time +2-3 weeks vs standard. MOQ 2 FCL.
