Quality

Why our briquette holds quality from Blora to Beirut.

Four operational choices we made and didn't back away from. They are the difference between a container that clears customs and one that arrives with humidity defects.

SOP · since day one

14-day minimum cooling at <40°C

Every batch sits a minimum 14 days under controlled temperature in open-air storage under roof before packing. Recorded per batch in our cooling reports. Result: stable burn, low ash, no surprise humidity defects on arrival.

Ingredients · three only

Natural binders. No chemicals.

Coconut charcoal powder, water, tapioca powder. That's the whole mix. Raw material sourced direct from private suppliers in Sulawesi — single-origin, single-grade, screened before milling.

Production · automated

Machine-consistent, every cycle.

Drying, screening, grinding, mixing, molding, oven — automatic line throughout. 12.5 ton/day total capacity. Oven runs 2.5 tons/cycle. Daily QC reports per supervisor.

Operation · direct manufacturer

200 employees. Visits welcome.

Factory at Jl. Raya Blora-Cepu KM. 14, Jiken — ~70 km from Tanjung Emas loading port. 200 employees, most from the Blora region. We're a manufacturer, not a trader. Plant tour open to serious buyers.

We don't compete on price. We compete on the boring stuff — clean documents, consistent batches, on-time loading — for the third, the fourth, the tenth container.

How we work

From first inquiry to fourth container.

01
Step

Inquiry → proforma in 24–48 hours.

Send specs: target volume, destination port, shape + size preference, packaging. We send a proforma at current FOB within one to two working days.

02
Step

Trial container, full doc set.

First-time buyers usually start with a single-FCL trial. DP 30–50% standard. Every document needed for customs clearance ships with the cargo — UN1361, MSDS, Phyto, COO, the lot.

03
Step

Repeat orders — reserved production slot.

Volume customers get a reserved production slot, fresh proforma per cycle, direct line to operations. References available for buyers running 4+ FCL/year.

04

BBQ-format briquette, custom shapes, custom packaging, brand printing — built to your spec. Lead time +2–3 weeks, MOQ typically 1–2 FCL depending on scope.
